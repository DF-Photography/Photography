Project 12: Late Night Drive

November 26th, 2025 — Trying to take pictures while in a moving car is one of the most difficult things to do. If I lower the shutter speed, the image is blurry. I raise the ISO and the image is noisy. My only option is to use a low aperture (f/2.8 and below). BUT MY LENS HAS A MINIMUM APERTURE OF F/3.5!!! I really need to invest in better quality lenses.
