Project 15: Downtown Chicago

January 20th, 2026 — I've been watching photography videos recently, tips on how to improve my photography. Two things stood out.
1. Study other photographers' works. What do I like about their photos?
2. Choose one or two genres. Don't be a jack-of-all-trades.
I made two decisions, to work on including shadows in my photos, and to continue with city/street photography.
