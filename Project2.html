Project 2: Sidewalk

April 28th, 2025 — During my photography class, we had an assignment where we had to take similar subjects and take the same pictures. Just changing out the subject. For that assignment I took photos of bridges. I wanted to do something similar so on my way to class I would take pictures of the path ahead of me before crossing the road.
