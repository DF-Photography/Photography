Project 16: Midday Walk

April 28th, 2026 — It’s been a while since I last had time to indulge in my hobby. During this time I watched more photography videos to improve my skills, even if I couldn’t put them into practice. During my brief hiatus I figured out manual mode. I would usually use the other three modes, thinking that manual was to advance for me. It wasn’t until I found a video that I realized that ISO doesn’t really matter. Yes, it does affect the noise of the image but if I set that to auto I can decide the shutter speed and aperture, the two factors that do affect how an image looks. I was already going to edit out the noise so I didn’t have to wait for it at the moment.
