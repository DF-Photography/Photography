Project 16: Midday Walk

April 28th, 2026 — It’s been a while since I last had time to indulge in my hobby. During this time I watched more photography videos to improve my skills, even if I couldn’t put them into practice. During my brief hiatus I figured out manual mode. I would usually use the other three modes, thinking that manual was to advance for me. It wasn’t until I found a video that I realized that ISO doesn’t really matter. Yes, it does affect the noise of the image but if I set that to auto I can decide the shutter speed and aperture, the two factors that do affect how an image looks. I was already going to edit out the noise so I didn’t have to worry about it at the moment.

Project 13: Zoo Lights

December 15th, 2025 — I learned my lesson from last time. The first thing I did was set my ISO to 100. I do not want to deal with noisy images later. I set my camera to aperture priority and chose f/3.5, the only thing I need to worry about is motion blur from 1"+ shutter speed. I thought "well, my subjects won't move and I brought my tripod." But I forgot to consider one thing, people. At first I was upset that people would walk into my shot but I then remembered something about the zoo. It used to be a cemetery. And there are rumors that it's haunted. Just assume that everyone is a ghost.
