Project 17: Downtown Chicago II

May 20th, 2026 — While I have tried to do street photography, the thought of someone spotting me and getting aggressive held me back. But it turns out that most people don’t even care. In fact, the people in the last image noticed me and made a comment on the architecture of the building. Hopefully I can have more of these types of interactions. I also improve my skills in color grading and color mixer. I used this to make my own presets for these images.
