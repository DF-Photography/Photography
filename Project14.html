Project 14: Old Orchard Mall

January 1st, 2025 — New year, new projects. Despite wanting to take new photos my job keeps getting in my way. I finally have time and am glad to do something I really enjoy. My family visited Old Orchard Mall in Skokie Illinois. I took the opportunity to take pictures in 13° weather. I hope to improve my skills in the following year.
